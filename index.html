About me

I’m a backend developer. Most of my work is APIs, authentication and database design, and getting that code running on a server.

On the backend I use Go, Express (Node.js) and FastAPI, with PostgreSQL or MongoDB for data. When a project needs a frontend I’ve worked with React, Next.js and Svelte. For deployment I mostly use AWS EC2 with Nginx, PM2 and Linux.

I went through the AWS re/Start program at AmaliTech, and I’m currently a systems engineer at Lytup, writing Go APIs for their battery systems.

Open to backend, cloud and DevOps roles
